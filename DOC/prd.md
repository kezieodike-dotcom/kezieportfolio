# 1. Product Overview

**Product Name:**
Chikezie Emmanuel Portfolio

**Product Type:**
Personal Portfolio Website

**Primary Goal:**
To showcase Chikezie Emmanuel’s expertise as a **Fullstack Software Developer and AI Systems Engineer**, highlight past projects, demonstrate technical capability, and attract clients, collaborators, and investors.

**Target Users**

1. Startup founders looking for a developer
2. Businesses needing digital platforms
3. Investors exploring technical partners
4. Recruiters looking for AI/fullstack engineers
5. Developers and collaborators

**Value Proposition**

The platform will present Chikezie as a **premium technical builder capable of transforming ideas into scalable products**, especially in:

* AI-powered applications
* Fullstack web platforms
* Web3 innovation systems
* MVP development for startups

The website should:

1. Establish credibility and authority
2. Showcase high-quality projects
